Actual responses will inform L3, never the exclusively desk-based L2.

Participation is voluntary. Institutional, service-level and individual contributions are accepted when their scope is clear. Respond directly to the assignment team, without prior approval by the forwarding NSO. Reporting is aggregated or anonymised by default; identifiable attribution or quotation follows your choice in the last section. Materials handed to the Client respect these permissions. A distinctive case may remain recognisable. Optional contact details are separate from analysis.

Do not provide political opinions, individual-level data, unnecessary personal details or confidential documents. Use public links or authorised non-confidential extracts. An individual contribution does not commit the institution.

A partial response is useful. No substantive option is preselected. A blank is neither no nor zero. None, do not know and not applicable exclude other answers where the questionnaire specifies.

Before submission, entries remain in the session, not in the database. They may be lost on closing: download a draft to resume. A draft is not a submission. Only a confirmed receipt attests to recording. Original responses are protected; only the consultant and authorised persons can access them. Changing language does not create a new contribution. If needed, use the fallback form in the same language and email it to the assignment contact.
